What You’ll Learn

  • Week 1-2: Introduction to modern strategy games (Catan, Ticket to Ride)
  • Week 3-4: Cooperative games and teamwork (Pandemic, Forbidden Island)
  • Week 5-6: Advanced resource management and tournament play

What Makes This Special

Board games are an incredible way to develop strategic thinking, negotiation skills, and patience without staring at a screen. Kids learn how to analyze situations, make decisions, and gracefully handle both winning and losing.
