Why Unskooling?

The world changed. Shouldn't how we learn change too?

  • ✗ One-size-fits-all curriculum

The New Way: Curiosity-Led Unskooling

Safety & Quality First

Your child's safety is non-negotiable. Here's how we ensure it.

🛡️

Locally Vetted Mentors

Every mentor is a verified teacher or resident from the city. We do multiple background checks and verify their credentials before they get onboarded.

👥

Open Community Sessions

All sessions happen in known & shared community spaces or society clubhouse. Parents are always welcome to observe. Full transparency.

⭐

Parent & Child Feedback Loop

After every session, parents & kids rate and review. Low-rated mentors are paused. Quality stays high.

📋

Trusted by the Community

Every mentor undergoes trainings on safety and child-friendly practices before they get onboarded. Community members further vet and approve them.
