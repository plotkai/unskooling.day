Mentors

Meet the passionate neighbors who teach at Unskooling.day

Anita Krishnan

Business & Entrepreneurship

Founder of two startups and a mentor at a leading accelerator, Anita is passionate about teaching kids to think like entrepreneurs. She runs 'Young Entrepreneurs Club' sessions where kids build and pitch mini-businesses within their own society.

📍 Sobha Dream Acres

Priya Sharma

Music (Guitar, Ukulele)

A software engineer at a top MNC by day and a passionate musician by evening. Priya has been playing guitar for 12 years and believes every child has a musical ear waiting to be awakened. She started teaching kids in her building's garden and now runs weekly sessions.

📍 Prestige Lakeside Habitat

Rahul Menon

Swimming & Water Sports

Former state-level swimmer from Kerala, Rahul moved to Bangalore 8 years ago. He's the unofficial swim coach of Brigade Gateway, having taught over 50 kids in the community pool. His focus on water safety makes him a parent favorite.
