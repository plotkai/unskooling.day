💭 Teaching Philosophy

I believe music isn’t about perfection — it’s about expression. When I teach kids, I don’t start with scales and theory. I start with a song they love. Once they hear themselves playing something familiar, the magic clicks.

My sessions are relaxed, fun, and full of laughter. We learn together, make mistakes together, and celebrate small victories together. By the end, kids don’t just know chords — they know they can learn anything they set their mind to.
