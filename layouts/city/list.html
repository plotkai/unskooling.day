Why Unskooling?

The world changed. Shouldn't how we learn change too?

📚

The Old Way: Rote Learning

  • ✗ Memorize, regurgitate, forget
  • ✗ One-size-fits-all curriculum
  • ✗ Learning disconnected from real life
  • ✗ Strangers teaching your kids far away
  • ✗ Expensive tuitions & coaching centres
🌻

The New Way: Curiosity-Led Unskooling

  • ✓ Learn by doing, exploring, creating
  • ✓ Personalized to your child's interests
  • ✓ Real skills from real practitioners
  • ✓ Neighbors you know & trust, 2 min walk
  • ✓ Affordable community-powered learning

How It Works

Three simple steps to unlock real-world learning in your community.

1

Parents or Society Signs Up

A society president or parent champion brings Unskooling to the community. We set up everything.

2

Skilled Teachers & Neighbors Become Mentors

Skilled teachers volunteer and handpicked by us to teach what they love — guitar, swimming, coding, business, art & more.

3

Kids Learn, Play & Grow in a Trusted Environment

Sessions happen in your society community hall, clubhouse, or park. Kids learn real skills in a safe place we all trust.

Safety & Quality First

Your child's safety is non-negotiable. Here's how we ensure it.

🛡️

Locally Vetted Mentors

Every mentor is a verified teacher or resident from the city. We do multiple background checks and verify their credentials before they get onboarded.

👥

Open Community Sessions

All sessions happen in known & shared community spaces or society clubhouse. Parents are always welcome to observe. Full transparency.

⭐

Parent & Child Feedback Loop

After every session, parents & kids rate and review. Low-rated mentors are paused. Quality stays high.

📋

Trusted by the Community

Every mentor undergoes trainings on safety and child-friendly practices before they get onboarded. Community members further vet and approve them.
