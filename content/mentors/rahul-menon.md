Water is my second home, and I want every child to feel that same comfort. Too many kids in apartments grow up afraid of water — that doesn't have to be the case when there's a pool 50 meters from their front door.

My approach is gentle but structured. Safety always comes first. I never rush a child into deep water. We build confidence gradually, one splash at a time. Parents tell me their kids go from crying at the pool edge to doing laps within a few weeks. That transformation is why I do this.
