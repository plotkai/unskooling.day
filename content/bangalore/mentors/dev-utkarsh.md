My first guitar was a ₹1500 instrument I bought from a local music shop when I was 12. I taught myself to play by watching YouTube tutorials and practicing for hours in my bedroom. By the time I was 18, I was playing in bands and performing at local venues.

Kids are natural musicians — they're curious, fearless, and creative. My job isn't to teach them music jargon. It's to channel that raw energy into structured thinking. By the end of my sessions, kids understand that music isn't just about playing notes — it's about expressing themselves and connecting with others.
