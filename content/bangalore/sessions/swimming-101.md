## What You'll Learn

- **Month 1:** Water safety, floating, and building water confidence
- **Month 2:** Freestyle stroke technique and breathing
- **Month 3:** Backstroke fundamentals and treading water

## Why Community Pool Learning Works

Kids learn best when they're comfortable. Your society pool is a familiar, safe environment — no intimidating Olympic-sized pools, no strangers. Just neighbors learning together with a qualified coach who lives in your building.

## Safety First
- Maximum 6 kids per batch
- Parents must be present for children under 8
- All sessions include 5-minute water safety briefing
- Coach is a certified lifeguard
