## What You'll Learn

- **Week 1-2:** Basic rhythms, counting beats, and simple steps
- **Week 3-4:** Coordination, body isolation, and learning a short routine
- **Week 5-6:** Expressions, energy, and refining movements
- **Week 7-8:** Performing a full choreographed routine together!

## What Makes This Special

This isn't a strict, formal dance class. It's about self-expression and having fun! We encourage kids to find their own style while learning the fundamentals of dance in a supportive, high-energy environment.

## What to Bring
- Comfortable workout clothes and sneakers
- A water bottle to stay hydrated
- Lots of energy! 💃🕺
