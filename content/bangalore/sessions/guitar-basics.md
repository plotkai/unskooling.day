## What You'll Learn

- **Week 1-2:** Basic chord shapes (C, G, Am, Em) and finger positioning
- **Week 3-4:** Strumming patterns for popular songs
- **Week 5-6:** Reading simple tablature and playing melodies
- **Week 7-8:** Performing your first complete song!

## What Makes This Special

This isn't a music class where kids sit in rows and follow a textbook. We learn by playing songs kids already love — Bollywood hits, nursery rhymes, and even anime themes. Every session ends with a mini jam session where kids play together.

## What to Bring
- A guitar (we have 2 loaners available for the first few sessions)
- A notebook for practice notes
- Enthusiasm! 🎸
