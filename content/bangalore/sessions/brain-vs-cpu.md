## What You'll Learn

- **Week 1-2:** Introduction to algorithms and logical sequences
- **Week 3-4:** Block-based coding and visual programming
- **Week 5-6:** Debugging, loops, and conditional statements
- **Week 7-8:** Building a simple interactive game or story

## What Makes This Special

We demystify programming by treating it like a puzzle. Kids won't just memorize syntax; they'll develop a computational mindset that helps them break down complex problems into manageable steps.

## What to Bring
- A laptop or tablet (we have a few spares if needed)
- Curiosity and a willingness to experiment! 💻
