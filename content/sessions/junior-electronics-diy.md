## What You'll Learn

- **Week 1-2:** Understanding circuits, power sources, and safety
- **Week 3-4:** Working with LEDs, switches, and resistors
- **Week 5-6:** Building your first motorized gadget or light-up craft!

## What Makes This Special

Hands-on building is the best way to understand how the world around us works. We provide safe, engaging kits that allow kids to see immediate, tangible results from their work, sparking a lifelong interest in engineering and science.

## What to Bring
- All electronics kits and safety equipment are provided.
- Just bring your imagination! ⚡
